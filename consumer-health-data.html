This Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy explains how Mário Otruba ("we", "us"), who makes and runs the STCKD app ("STCKD: Peptides & Supplements"), handles consumer health data. It is written for people covered by the Washington My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373), Nevada's consumer health data law (Senate Bill 370, NRS Chapter 603A) and the consumer health data rules of the Connecticut Data Privacy Act. We apply it to everyone in the United States.

It adds to our Privacy Policy, which covers all personal data.

1. Summary

  • You can use STCKD without an account. Then your health data stays on your phone and we never receive it.
  • If you have an account, we store your health data on our servers only after you give a separate consent. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • We use it only to store it, sync it between your devices and show it back to you.
  • We do not share it with third parties or affiliates. We do not sell it. We do not use geofencing.
  • You can see, export and delete your data in the app, or by writing to support@stckd.app.

3. Consumer health data we collect

"Consumer health data" means personal information that is linked or reasonably linkable to you and identifies your past, present or future physical or mental health. In STCKD, this is what you choose to track:

We use this data only to store it, sync it between your devices, restore it, and show it back to you. The app's insights, charts and reports are calculated on your phone.

We do not collect precise location, biometric data, genetic data, or data about reproductive or sexual health care. We do not try to infer health information about you from other data.

4. Where the data comes from

  • From you: everything in the table above is what you type or tap into the app.
  • From your device: the app adds technical sync information, such as when your data was last saved.
  • From Google, only if you sign in with Google: your Google email, name, profile-picture link and account identifier.

We do not buy or receive consumer health data from anyone else.

5. Your consent

Collection. Data you track in guest mode stays on your phone, and we never receive it. If you have an account, the app asks for your consent before it sends your health data to our servers. This consent is separate from our Terms, and the box is not ticked for you. If you do not consent, the app keeps working, but your data stays only on your phone.

Sharing. We do not share consumer health data with third parties. If we ever wanted to, we would first ask for your separate consent, as the law requires.

Withdrawing consent. In the app, open Profile (tap your avatar on the Today screen) → Legal & privacy → Withdraw health-data consent. You can also email support@stckd.app. Syncing stops, and we delete the synced copy from our servers. It disappears from backups within up to 7 days after deletion (Supabase daily backups roll over). Your data stays on your phone.

6. Who receives your data

Third parties and affiliates: none. We do not share consumer health data with any third party, and we have no affiliates.

Processors. These companies handle data only on our behalf, under contract and on our instructions. The law does not treat them as third parties:

7. No sale and no geofencing

We do not sell consumer health data. We do not collect your location, and we do not use geofences around health care facilities or anywhere else.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data;
  • access it and get a copy, together with a list of every third party and affiliate that received it. Today this list is empty. We also name our processors and how to contact them;
  • delete it. This includes the synced copy on our servers, and we make sure our processors delete it too. Backups roll over within up to 7 days after deletion (Supabase daily backups roll over);
  • withdraw your consent to our collection (section 5);
  • in Connecticut, also correct your data, get it in a portable format, and opt out of sale, targeted advertising and profiling. We do none of these;
  • appeal if we turn down your request (section 10).

We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

9. How to use your rights

  • In the app: export your data (Profile → Export my data (JSON)), edit or delete entries, withdraw consent, or delete your account (Profile → Delete account & all data). See Delete Your Account and Data.
  • By email: write to support@stckd.app, ideally from the email address of your account.

We check that the request comes from you. Usually a message from your account's email address is enough. Otherwise we may ask you to confirm from that address or to sign in to the app. We never ask for your password. Someone else can make a request for you if you give them written permission.

We answer within 45 days. If we need more time, we may extend this once by another 45 days and will tell you why. Requests are free.

10. Appeals

If we turn down your request, you can appeal. Reply to our answer, or email support@stckd.app with the subject "Health data appeal". We answer in writing within 45 days and explain our decision.

If we deny your appeal, you can complain to your state's Attorney General:

  • Washington: Washington State Attorney General, atg.wa.gov
  • Nevada: Nevada Attorney General, ag.nv.gov
  • Connecticut: Connecticut Attorney General, portal.ct.gov/ag

11. Security and access

Only Mário Otruba has administrator access to our systems. Our processors must keep your data confidential. How we protect data is explained in section 19 of our Privacy Policy.

12. Changes

When we change this policy, we update the version and dates at the top and tell you in the app about important changes. We will ask for your consent before we collect or use consumer health data for a new purpose. Previous versions are in our archive.
