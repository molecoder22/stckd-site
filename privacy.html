1. Summary

  • You can use STCKD without an account. Then everything you track stays on your iPhone and never reaches us.

  • STCKD is for adults (18+) only.
  • Questions: support@stckd.app.

3.1 What you track

This is the information you type or tap into the app:

  • Supplements, peptides and medicines: name, category (for example peptide, GLP-1 or TRT), dose and unit, method (for example subcutaneous or intramuscular), time, schedule, cycles and pauses, half-life, vial inventory and how much is left.
  • Stacks: their names, colors, schedules and which items they contain.
  • Intake history: which doses you took or skipped, when, and the actual dose if it differed.
  • Injection sites: the site of each dose and your rotation settings.
  • Feel Log: mood, energy (1 to 10), a daily note, and symptoms with their severity.
  • Body weight and your preferred unit.
  • Notes you write, for example about a brand, a batch or where a product came from.
  • Your focus choice (peptides, GLP-1, supplements or all) and your reminder and display settings.
  • Sync information: when your data was last saved, which items you deleted, and whether you finished the tutorial.

Much of this is health data. Section 5 explains how we protect it.

Where it is stored. It is always stored on your iPhone, inside the app. If you use the app as a guest, it never leaves your iPhone and we never receive it. If you have an account and have given your health-data consent, a copy is also stored in our database (run by Supabase in the United States) as one record linked to your account.

If you used the app as a guest and then sign in, the data already on your iPhone is added to your account and synced, once you have given that consent.

3.2 Your account

If you create an account, we receive:

  • Email sign-up: your email address and your password. Our database provider stores only a scrambled (hashed) form of your password.
  • Sign in with Apple: an Apple account identifier and an email address. If you choose "Hide My Email", this is a private relay address that Apple creates. Apple may also offer your name to the app. The app does not send it to our servers.
  • Sign in with Google: your Google email address, your name, a link to your Google profile picture, and a Google account identifier. Google always sends these when you sign in with Google. Our database provider stores them with your account, and they are deleted when you delete your account. While you sign in, Google's own sign-in software inside the app may also collect technical information for Google's purposes, such as your IP address (which can show your approximate location, used against fraud), a device identifier and usage data. Google's privacy policy covers that; we do not receive it.
  • A user ID that our database creates to link your data to your account.

The app has no screen for changing your email address. If you need to change it, write to support@stckd.app.

3.3 Security and technical data

Whenever the app talks to our servers (only while you are signed in), our provider Supabase automatically records technical data. This includes your IP address, device and app details (the "user agent"), the times you sign in and make requests, your session tokens, and a log of sign-in events. We use this to keep your account secure, stop abuse and fix problems.

3.4 Terms and consent records

When you create an account, we store the date and time, the versions of the Terms and this Privacy Policy you accepted, and your user ID. When you give or withdraw your health-data consent, we store the same kind of record. These records let us show that you agreed.

3.5 Messages to support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. Our mailbox runs on Google Workspace. Please do not include health details unless we need them to help you.

3.6 Data that stays on your iPhone

Some data is stored only on your iPhone. We never receive it:

  • Age confirmation: that you confirmed you are 18 or older, and when. We do not ask for your date of birth.
  • "How STCKD works" acknowledgement, and when you gave it.
  • Calculator entries, such as vial size, water volume and price.
  • Reminders. They are local notifications that your iPhone schedules itself. There is no push server. "Private reminders" is on by default, so the lock screen shows only "STCKD" and no names or doses. You can change this in Profile.
  • Your STCKD Pro status. The app asks Apple, on your iPhone, whether your subscription is active. This status is not sent to our servers.
  • Rating-prompt timing: when you first opened the app and whether we already asked you to rate it, so Apple's rating prompt is not shown too often.
  • Everything you track in guest mode.

Signing out or deleting your account removes your tracked data from that iPhone. The age confirmation, calculator entries and rating-prompt timing stay until you delete the app.

3.7 Files you create and share

  • Data export (JSON): a file with everything you tracked plus your account email.
  • Monthly report (PDF, STCKD Pro): a summary of the last 30 days.
  • Share card: an image of your stack.

All three are made on your iPhone. They leave your iPhone only if you share them, and they go only where you choose. Temporary copies are deleted after you share. To save a share card to Photos, the app asks for "add only" access, so it cannot see your photos. Once you send a file to another app or person, their own rules apply to it.

Apple (merchant of record for all in-app purchases). Apple handles your payment details. We never see them. Apple gives us sales and payout reports, and these do not identify you.

The password-reset page is the only page that talks to our servers. It sends your new password to Supabase, and only when you open a valid reset link. The reset session is kept only in the page's memory and is gone when you close it.

3.11 What we do not collect

We do not collect your date of birth, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, face or fingerprint data, or your advertising identifier. STCKD does not connect to Apple Health: your weight is only what you type in. The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or session-recording tools.

5. Health data and your consent

What you track about compounds, doses, injection sites, symptoms, mood, energy and weight is health data. The GDPR calls it "data concerning health". US state laws call it "sensitive" or "consumer health" data.

We store it on our servers only with your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)). When you create an account, the app asks for this consent separately from the Terms. The box is not ticked for you. This consent also serves as the consent required by US state health-data laws and as express consent under Canadian law.

What the consent covers: storing a copy of your tracked data in our database (Supabase, United States) so it syncs between your devices and can be restored. Nothing else.

If you do not consent, you can still use every feature on your iPhone. Your data simply stays there and is not synced.

How to withdraw: in the app, open Profile (tap your avatar on the Today screen) → Legal & privacy → Withdraw health-data consent. You can also email support@stckd.app. When you withdraw:

  • syncing stops straight away;
  • we delete the synced copy from our database, and it disappears from backups within up to 7 days after deletion (Supabase daily backups roll over);
  • your data stays on your iPhone;
  • your account stays, so you can give consent again later if you want.

Deleting your account also ends your consent.

What we never do with health data: we do not use it for advertising, marketing, research or data mining. We do not sell it, share it or show it to anyone. We do not analyze it to draw conclusions about your health. The Insights, the estimated-level chart and the monthly report are simple calculations that run on your iPhone. STCKD is a tracking tool, not medical advice. See our Health Disclaimer.

6. No artificial intelligence

STCKD uses no artificial intelligence or machine learning, on your iPhone or on our servers. We do not send your data to any AI service, and we do not use it to train any model. We make no automated decisions about you and do no profiling.

8. Transfers outside the EU

We are based in the EU, but our providers store and process data in the United States and other countries. Here is how each transfer is protected:

19. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, so here is exactly what we do:

  • Encrypted connections: the app talks to our servers only over HTTPS. Our website is served over HTTPS.
  • Access rules: database rules (Supabase "row-level security") let each signed-in account read and change only its own data.
  • Encryption at rest: our database provider, Supabase, states that it encrypts all stored customer data (AES-256). Your data is not end-to-end encrypted. This means our provider's systems and our administrator account can technically read it. We look at it only when we need to run the service, fix a problem you report, or follow the law.
  • Your session: on your iPhone, the token that keeps you signed in is stored in the iOS Keychain and is available only on that iPhone.
  • Lock screen and app switcher: "Private reminders" is on by default. The app hides its screen in the iPhone app switcher.
  • On your iPhone: your tracked data is stored inside the app. We do not add our own encryption to it. It relies on your iPhone's own protection, such as your passcode. It may also be included in your iPhone backups (iCloud or computer).
  • Limited access: only Mário Otruba has administrator access.

Please use a strong, unique password and keep your iPhone locked with a passcode.

If it is likely to put you at high risk, we will tell you directly without undue delay.

This includes telling you within 60 days of discovering the breach, and telling the FTC and, where the rule requires, the media within the rule's deadlines. We will also follow any state breach-notification laws that apply.

For Québec, we will report incidents that risk serious injury to the Commission d'accès à l'information and tell the people affected.

We keep a record of every breach, even small ones.
