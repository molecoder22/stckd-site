STCKD Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

STCKD ("the app") is a supplement tracking app operated by Mário Otruba. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, used solely to create and secure your account (email/password or Sign in with Apple).
  • App content you create: your supplements, stacks, schedules, intake history, and mood/energy log entries.

How it's stored

Your data is stored on your device and, when you're signed in, synced to our database hosted by Supabase. Data is protected by row-level security: only your authenticated account can read or write your records. We do not sell your data, show ads, or share your data with third parties for marketing.

Health disclaimer

STCKD is a tracking tool, not a medical service. Nothing in the app is medical advice. Log entries and supplement records are self-reported by you and are never analyzed for medical purposes.

Data deletion

You can delete your content in the app at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at support@stckd.app and we will remove it within 30 days.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through your App Store account. We never see or store your payment details.
