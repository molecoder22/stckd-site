4. What STCKD is, and what it is not

STCKD is a personal record-keeping and reminder app. It lets you:

  • keep your own log of supplements, peptides and other compounds you already take;
  • set reminders at the times you choose;
  • track what is left in a vial or pack;
  • do reconstitution arithmetic on numbers you type in;
  • record how you feel, symptoms and body weight, and see your own entries over time.

STCKD is not medical advice and not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent anything. It does not recommend, check or adjust any dose. It does not tell you what to take, and it does not sell or supply any substance. Every compound, dose and schedule in the app comes from you. The calculator only does arithmetic on your numbers. The estimated-level chart is an educational estimate, not a blood level.

Talk to a qualified healthcare professional before you start, stop, change or combine anything. Read the full Health Disclaimer.
